Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2025  |  Last Updated: May 19, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sac Sangat mobile application, available on Android and iOS. Please read this policy to understand our practices.

🛡️

No Data Collection

Sac Sangat does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data from its users — of any kind.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, usage statistics, or any other information.

Your privacy is fully preserved. Nothing leaves your device.
📊

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any analytics tools, crash reporting services, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking libraries. There is no tracking of any kind within the app.

🌐

Internet & Permissions

Sac Sangat operates entirely offline. The app does not make any network requests and does not require an internet connection to function. Any device permissions requested are used solely for local app functionality (such as microphone access for speech features) and no data from these permissions is transmitted externally.

👶

Children's Privacy

Because we collect no data whatsoever, our app is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We fully comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations.

🔄

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is ever updated, the new version will be posted within the app and on the relevant app store listing. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
