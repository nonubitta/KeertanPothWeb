Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's a straightforward explanation of what information the game uses and how it is handled.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

👋 Overview

Name Place Animal Thing ("the App") is a multiplayer word game. The App is designed to work without requiring an account or collecting personal information.

🔒 Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personally identifiable information. You can use the App without providing your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or other personal information.

Personal information Not collected
Location Not collected
Contacts Not collected
Cloud accounts Not required

📡 Multiplayer

The App allows players to connect directly over the same local Wi-Fi network. This connection is used to exchange the information required to run the multiplayer game.

Local multiplayer stays local.
Player names, answers, scores, and game status are exchanged between participating devices and are not uploaded to our servers.

📱 Device Permissions

The App may request network-related permissions needed to discover and communicate with other devices on the local network. These permissions are used only for multiplayer functionality.

📊 Analytics & Advertising

The App does not currently use third-party advertising or analytics services that collect personal information.

💾 Data Storage

Game information may be temporarily stored on your device while playing. We do not maintain a cloud database containing your personal information or game activity.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. No account or personal information is required to play the game.

🤝 Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page together with an updated date.

✉️ Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact information provided on the App's store listing or website.
