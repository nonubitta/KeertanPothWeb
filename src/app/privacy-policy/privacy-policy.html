Overview

Thank you for using Keertan Pothi. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Keertan Pothi app and website collect and how that information is used.

Keertan Pothi is designed to collect only limited technical information needed to maintain and improve the app. We do not collect personal information to identify you.

Information We Collect

Keertan Pothi may collect limited, non-personal technical information related to the app and its content database, such as:

  • The version of the Keertan Pothi app installed on your device
  • The version of the Keertan Pothi database currently installed on your device
  • Information indicating whether a database update is available or has been applied
  • Other basic technical information necessary to identify app or database compatibility

This information is used to understand which app and database versions are in use, provide appropriate updates, troubleshoot compatibility issues, and improve the reliability of Keertan Pothi.

Information We Do Not Collect

Keertan Pothi does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address or phone number
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Precise location information
  • Photos, videos, or personal files
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Personal messages or communications
  • Payment or financial information
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking purposes

The technical information described above is not intended to identify individual users.

How We Use Technical Information

The limited technical information we collect may be used to:

  • Determine which app versions are currently in use
  • Determine which database versions are currently in use
  • Provide and manage database updates
  • Identify compatibility issues between app and database versions
  • Improve app stability, performance, and reliability
  • Understand general adoption of app and database versions

We do not use this information to build personal profiles or track individual users.

Local Data Storage

Keertan Pothi may store information locally on your device, including preferences, bookmarks, history, downloaded content, and other app-related data. This information is stored on your device to provide app functionality and is not collected as personal information by Keertan Pothi.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The limited non-personal technical information described in this policy may be processed by infrastructure or service providers used to operate Keertan Pothi, such as hosting or server infrastructure, solely as necessary to provide and maintain the service.

Third-Party Services

Keertan Pothi does not use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking services. We may use third-party infrastructure or service providers to host APIs, databases, or other technical services required for the operation of the app and website.

Any information processed through these services is limited to what is necessary for the operation and maintenance of Keertan Pothi.

Children's Privacy

Keertan Pothi is intended for users of all ages. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users. The limited technical information described in this policy is not intended to identify an individual user.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited technical information collected through Keertan Pothi. However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, website, or our data practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
